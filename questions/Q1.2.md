# Q1.2: Syntactic restrictions for PTIME implication

Parent question: [Q1: Complexity of implication for disjunctions of dependence atoms](Q1.md).

## Question

Which syntactic restrictions on the premises and consequent guarantee a
polynomial-time decision procedure for implication for disjunctions of
dependence atoms?

The value domain remains unrestricted. Complexity is measured in the
total length of the explicitly represented input formulas. Finite-team
and unrestricted implication coincide on these fragments as well.

[Q1.1](Q1.1.md) asks the broader question of which restrictions guarantee
decidability.
