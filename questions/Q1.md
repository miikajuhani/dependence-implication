# Q1: Complexity of implication for disjunctions of dependence atoms

## Question

What is the exact complexity of the implication problem for disjunctions of dependence atoms?

## Answer

The problem is **co-r.e.-complete under computable many-one reductions**.
Finite-team and unrestricted implication coincide, so the classification
applies to both variants. In particular, the problem is undecidable.
